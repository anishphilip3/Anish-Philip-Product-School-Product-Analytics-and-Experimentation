The Bet · 07
Why this loop

It fixes both broken numbers.

  • Conversion: more stakeholders in the workspace = more value felt = more reason to pay.
  • Retention: multi-seat accounts carry real switching cost — they churn far less.
  • Not Content or Referral: those feed a top of funnel that already doesn't convert or retain.
The Bet · 08
The bet, formalized
FinWise is stuck in single-player mode — trials convert at a flat ~2% and the paid base is net shrinking. The highest-leverage first experiment is a Collaboration loop: invite a teammate or accountant, and measure trial→paid conversion and multi-seat retention.
