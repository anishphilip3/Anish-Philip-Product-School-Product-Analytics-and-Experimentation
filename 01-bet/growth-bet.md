# The Bet · FinWise

> Module 1 · Ignite a PLG Motion. The growth hypothesis and where you're betting, plus the growth-loop visualization.

## Growth hypothesis

If we add an in-trial milestone guiding users to FinWise's core value action (connect data → first modeling output), then trial→paid conversion rises ~1–2pp (from 2% toward 3–4%), because a 2% rate means most users never hit the Aha moment before the paywall, measured by an A/B test over ~4–6 weeks — primary: conversion; guardrail: early paid churn.

_____

## The bet

Bet on a **Collaboration growth loop**, not acquisition. FinWise is stuck in single-player mode — trials convert at a flat ~2% and the paid base is net shrinking (317 churned vs 128 new paid over 13 months). More top-of-funnel spend no longer moves the needle.

**Where we focus:** make FinWise multiplayer. Owner invites accountant/bookkeeper → they manage books together → more seats join → FinWise becomes the system of record → hits limits → upgrades → each collaborator seeds a new workspace → loop restarts.

**What we're deliberately NOT doing:** Content loops or Referral loops — those feed a top of funnel that already doesn't convert or retain. The bottleneck is downstream of acquisition.
