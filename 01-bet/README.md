# 01 · The Bet — Ignite a PLG Motion (Module 1)

**Deliverable:** FinWise's growth hypothesis & bet, plus the growth-loop visualization.

## The hypothesis

> **IF** we add an in-trial activation milestone that guides new reverse-trial users to complete FinWise's core value action (connect data → generate a first financial insight),
> **THEN** trial→paid conversion **WILL increase BY** ~1–2 percentage points (from 2% toward 3–4%),
> **BECAUSE** a 2% conversion rate signals most users never hit the "aha" moment before the paywall lands,
> **MEASURED BY** an A/B test over ~4–6 weeks — primary metric trial→paid conversion, guardrail early paid churn.

## The evidence (from the 13-month funnel)

- **The funnel:** 94,558 visits → 6,424 trials (6.8%) → 128 paid (**2.0%**). The steep cliff is **Trial→Paid** (98% of trial-starters never pay).
- **Conversion is pinned at ~2%** every single month regardless of trial volume — a structural bottleneck, not a volume one.
- **Acquisition is tapped out** — more spend no longer moves growth, so the leak is *downstream* of acquisition.
- **Retention is co-primary:** total churn (317) far outran new paid (128) over 13 months — the paid base is net shrinking.

## The bet: a Collaboration growth loop

Of Content-Driven / Referral / Collaboration, the **Collaboration loop** is the pick — it attacks *both* failing numbers: pulling an accountant/bookkeeper/teammate into the workspace deepens activation (lifting the 2% conversion) **and** creates multi-seat dependency (fixing churn > new-paid).

```
   ┌────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┐
   ▼                                                        │
TRIGGER: Owner starts trial, invited to add accountant/     │
         bookkeeper/co-founder                               │
   ▼                                                        │
STAGE 1: Collaborator joins → they manage the books together │
   ▼                                                        │
STAGE 2: They feel shared financial visibility → invite more │
   ▼                                                        │
STAGE 3: More seats join → FinWise becomes system of record  │
   ▼                                                        │
OUTCOME: Account hits limits → upgrades; each collaborator ──┘
         can seed a new workspace (loop restarts, bigger base)
```

## Formalized bet

> The biggest growth problem is that FinWise is stuck in **single-player mode** — trials convert at a flat ~2% and the paid base is net shrinking — because value is experienced once, not repeatedly. The highest-leverage first experiment is a **Collaboration loop**: prompt trial users to invite a teammate/accountant, and measure trial→paid conversion and multi-seat retention.
