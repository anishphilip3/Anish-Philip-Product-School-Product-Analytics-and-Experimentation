Product Analytics & Experimentation
Part 1 · why this experiment

Module 4 said the pool is capped.

  • Import Usage sits at a ~40% ceiling — the gateway to the North Star is stuck. Users who never connect data never see value.
  • The connect-a-bank wall is the single biggest friction point before the Aha. Not everyone is ready to hand over bank credentials on day one.
  • The fix: offer an "Explore with sample data" path — let users feel the Aha before asking them to connect real data.

The Validation · 05
The experiment spec

Four fields, pre-set.

Objective
Lift activated trial users (import + first modeling output) by removing the connect-a-bank wall.
Primary metric
% of new trial users reaching first modeling output within 7 days.
Success threshold
Significant (p < 0.05), ≥5pp absolute lift vs control. Read at a pre-set ~6 week date. Extend rather than peek.
Guardrail
Trial→Paid conversion must not significantly drop — guards the vanity-activation trap.

If successful: ship → Holdout for the retention tail → open the pricing experiment (Module 6). If not: instrument Get-Started to find the true drop-off, or pivot to pricing.

The Validation · 07
The four lenses

All four flash amber.

⚠ Significance
p ≈ 0.36 (χ² = 0.83). Not significant at n=20 — too small to trust either direction.
⚠ Practical (MDE)
At n=10/group the MDE is ~30pp — we can't detect a real difference this small.
⚠ Confidence interval
95% CI spans [−15pp, +55pp] — includes zero and massive effects. Too wide.
⚠ Guardrail
Conversion is the metric that pays. B trades it for session time — engagement only +1.6pp.
The Validation · 08
The scenario & the call

Mixed Signal → Investigate.

Mixed Signal→Investigate
1
Re-run at proper power
n=10/group is noise. Target ~500/arm to detect a 5pp conversion difference.
2
Isolate the driver
B bundled CTA + design changes. Test B's design with A's CTA to find which moved what.
3
Segment & measure downstream
Organic converts higher than Paid in both groups. Check if B's longer sessions → better week-4 retention.
The Validation · 09
The discipline
Design the experiment before you need the answer. Read the result with no attachment. Ship, iterate, or kill — but never ignore the guardrail.
