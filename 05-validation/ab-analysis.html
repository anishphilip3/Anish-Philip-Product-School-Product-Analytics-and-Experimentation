Reading the numbers — the four lenses
Applied before any ship / kill / iterate decision
⚠ Significance
Conversion gap (70% vs 50%) yields p ≈ 0.36 (χ² = 0.83, df=1). Not significant at n=20 — the sample is far too small to trust either direction.
⚠ Practical (vs MDE)
A 20pp gap looks large, but at n=10/group the MDE is ~30pp — we can't detect a real difference this small. Session gap (+27s) is similarly underpowered.
⚠ Confidence interval
Conversion diff 95% CI spans roughly [−15pp, +55pp] — it includes zero and massive effects. The interval is too wide to act on.
⚠ Guardrail
Conversion is the metric that pays. B trades conversion for session time — but engagement is only +1.6pp, barely a signal. Guardrail bends.
The scenario & the call
Mixed Signal→Investigate
The primary business metric (conversion) and the secondary (session duration) point in opposite directions, but neither is statistically significant at n=20. Shipping B trades the metric that pays for one that doesn't — yet. Killing B throws away a session-time signal worth understanding. The disciplined move is to re-run at proper power (~500/arm) and isolate the engagement driver before deciding.
The answer
Support or refute?Inconclusive / refuted as stated. B was meant to win both metrics — it shows longer sessions but lower conversion (70% vs 50%). Feature engagement is roughly flat (+1.6pp). However, neither difference is significant at n=20.
Which scenario?Mixed Signal — A converts more, B has longer sessions; but the sample is too small for either gap to be trustworthy.
RecommendationKeep A live as the conversion baseline (protect the revenue metric) and investigate — do not roll out B on session time alone, and don't kill a possible engagement signal either.
Next steps① Re-run at proper power — n=10/group is noise; target ~500/arm to detect a 5pp conversion difference. ② Isolate the driver — B bundled two changes (CTA + design); test B's design with A's CTA. ③ Segment by traffic source — Organic users convert higher in both groups; Paid users barely convert. ④ Measure downstream — does B's longer sessions → better week-4 retention?
