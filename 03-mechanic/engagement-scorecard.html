Interpret & iterate. FinWise's baseline lands at 2.6 / 10 — Needs work. The heaviest drag is Retention (churn), and the biggest actionable lever behind it is the collaboration gap — the exact bet Modules 1–2 pointed to. Churn is an outcome, the engagement dials are the inputs that move it. Revisit these weights as priorities shift; a scorecard that never updates is just a snapshot.
On the numbers: weights reflect each metric's retention correlation — collaboration, feature adoption and churn weighted highest; DAU and session duration lowest (for a weekly-cadence finance tool, daily use and long sessions are weak signals). Churn is scored inverse (low churn = high score) and sits in its own Retention dimension because it's the outcome the other dials predict. Baseline scores are informed estimates anchored to Module 1 signals (Sessions/User 3–9, Import-Data ~30–45%, 60% one-year churn, single-player usage) and should be replaced with real product analytics.
