## The mechanic: "Money Momentum" — a forgiving weekly streak

Reinforces the **weekly money-review habit** (open FinWise → review the updated snapshot → act on ≥1 insight) — the recurring form of the Aha, and the behavior that predicts retention.

**Why a *forgiving weekly* streak (a "something new" design):**
- **Weekly unit**, not daily — money moves weekly; a daily streak would nag and break on normal life.
- **Grace week** auto-banked monthly → one missed week never resets months of habit (defuses the punishing/manipulative anti-pattern).
- **Rewards are deeper insight, not points:** Wk4 → Monthly Trend Report · Wk12 → Quarterly Cash-Flow Forecast · Wk26 → Half-Year Review · Wk52 → Year in Review.
- **Identity + one well-timed nudge** (never notification spam).

**Motivation tapped:** loss aversion + owner identity (the Hook Model's *investment*), with a secondary progress/goal-gradient. Connected to core value because the protected "progress" is financial clarity and the rewards are real insight.

## Diagnose & Defend (vs the 60% churn)

**Diagnosis:** the churn is **value/habit-related, not price** — it's a reverse trial (value already seen) yet 60% lapse, while engagement is shallow/inconsistent. A one-time Aha that never becomes routine = a habit/value-continuity gap.
**Defense:** Money Momentum converts the one-time Aha into a recurring weekly behavior (external trigger → internal habit) and accumulates personalized reports (an *investment* that raises switching cost). **Limit:** it won't fix *price*-driven churn or organizational lock-in — so it pairs with the Module 1 collaboration loop and the Module 6 pricing model.

## The engagement scorecard (how we'd measure it)

A weighted engagement KPI across depth / breadth / frequency / retention, weighted by retention correlation. FinWise's baseline lands at **2.6 / 10 (Needs work)**; the mechanic is designed to move **Weekly Return (3→7)** and **Insight Engagement (3→7)** as leading indicators, with churn as the lagging outcome.
