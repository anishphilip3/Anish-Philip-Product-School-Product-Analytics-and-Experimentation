The Mechanic · 02
The diagnosis · why people really leave

It's a value gap, not a price problem.

Engagement is shallow
All four dimensions — Depth, Breadth, Frequency, Retention — sit at 1–3 out of 10.
Value stops after week 1
Users who see the initial snapshot return less each week — the Aha decays without reinforcement.
Price is not the objection
It's a reverse trial — users see full value first. They leave because the value stopped being new, not because it cost too much.
The Mechanic · 03
Why weekly

Money moves weekly. A daily streak would break.

  • Financial transactions batch weekly — payroll, invoices, reconciliation all run on a weekly cycle.
  • Daily check-ins would be noise — most business accounts don't change every day. Daily nagging means "nothing to see."
  • Weekly matches the natural cadence — the behavior we want is "review your money picture once a week and act on one insight."
The Mechanic · 04
Why forgiving

One missed week should never erase months of habit.

Punishing streak (the anti-pattern)
Miss one day → reset to zero. Hard streaks cause anxiety, resentment, and rage-quits. A finance tool using this pattern feels manipulative — "you lost your 47-week streak because you were on holiday" is a betrayal, not motivation.
Forgiving streak (Money Momentum)
Grace week auto-banked monthly. One free pass accumulates each month. Miss a week? The grace covers it — no reset, no guilt. The streak protects months of real progress instead of punishing normal life.

This is the "something new" design: it keeps the motivational power of streaks (loss aversion, progress) while removing the punishing edge that drives people away.

The Mechanic · 06
Why not other mechanics

We rejected leaderboards and points.

No
Leaderboard
Financial data is deeply private. Ranking users against each other on money behavior is a trust violation — it pushes disclosure no one wants and rewards comparison, not clarity.
No
Points / badges
Extrinsic rewards decouple the mechanic from real value. When points stop feeling novel, the habit collapses. We want the reward to be the insight itself.
Yes
Forgiving streak + value milestones
Loss aversion protects real progress, and each milestone unlocks a genuinely useful financial report — the reward IS the product value.
The Mechanic · 07
Why it works · the psychology

Three forces pulling in the same direction.

  • Loss aversion: once you've built a 12-week streak, the thought of losing it is more powerful than the thought of earning the next milestone.
  • Owner identity: each week of financial data reviewed becomes part of "my money picture" — the more you invest, the harder it is to walk away (the Hook Model's Investment).
  • Goal gradient: "3 more weeks to Quarterly Forecast" — as you get closer, motivation accelerates.

The Mechanic · 09
The honest defense
Money Momentum converts the one-time Aha into a recurring weekly behavior and accumulates reports that raise switching cost. It won't fix price-driven churn or lock-in — so it pairs with the collaboration loop (Module 1) and pricing (Module 6).
