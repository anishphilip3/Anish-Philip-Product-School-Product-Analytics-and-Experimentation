# The Mechanic · FinWise

> Module 3 · Retention & Engagement. The engagement mechanic that builds the habit loop, with rationale and a wireframe.

## The mechanic

**"Money Momentum"** — a forgiving weekly streak with value milestones.

- **Trigger:** weekly push notification ("Your money moved this week")
- **Action:** open FinWise → review weekly snapshot → act on ≥1 insight
- **Reward:** deeper financial insight that only unlocks with sustained use (4-week: Monthly Trend, 12-week: Quarterly Forecast, 26-week: Half-Year Review, 52-week: Year in Review)
- **Investment:** each completed week builds a streak that users don't want to lose; a grace week auto-banks monthly (miss a week, no reset, no guilt)

_____

## Rationale

60% of paying customers churn within 12 months. Engagement KPI sits at 2.6/10. The problem isn't price — it's that value stops being new after week 1.

We rejected **leaderboards** (financial data is private — ranking users on money behavior is a trust violation) and **points/badges** (extrinsic rewards decouple from real value; when points stop feeling novel, the habit collapses).

Money Momentum works because it aligns three psychological forces: **loss aversion** (protect the streak), **owner identity** (each week of reviewed data becomes "my money picture"), and **goal gradient** ("3 more weeks to Quarterly Forecast" accelerates motivation). The mechanic matches the weekly cadence of financial activity — daily streaks would break because most business accounts don't change every day.
