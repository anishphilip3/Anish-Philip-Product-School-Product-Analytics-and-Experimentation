# The Solution · FinWise

> Module 2 · Acquisition & Activation. The onboarding solution and the Aha moment that makes value land.

## Aha moment

A user **connects their data** and sees their first personalized financial snapshot — one plain-language insight about their money. Not a feature: a realization. It predicts retention because financial visibility is a recurring need, so a user who reaches it has a built-in reason to return.

_____

## Onboarding prototype

[View the onboarding prototype →](onboarding-prototype.html)

Five screens, three to value:
1. **Welcome** — tap your goal (personalizes the path)
2. **Connect** — connect a bank **or** use sample data (the key friction removal)
3. **Snapshot** ✨ — see your first insight (the Aha)
4. **Invite** — bring in your accountant (post-Aha)
5. **Success** — Money Momentum begins

_____

## Why this activates

The drop is at **getting data in** — only ~39% of trial users ever import data, and that ceiling has sat at ~40% for 13 months. The solution adds a **"sample-data on-ramp"**: users who aren't ready to connect a real bank can explore with sample data and reach a modeling output immediately. Value first, asks later — one job per screen.

**Primary metric:** % of new trial users who reach their first modeling output within 7 days (today ~37%).
**Guardrail:** trial→paid conversion must not drop.
