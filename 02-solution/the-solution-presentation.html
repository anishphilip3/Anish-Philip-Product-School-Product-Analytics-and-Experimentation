The Solution · 02
The Aha moment
A user connects their data and sees their first personalized snapshot — one plain-language insight about their money. Not a feature: a realization — "I can finally see my business at a glance."

Why it predicts retention: financial visibility is a recurring need, so a user who reaches it has a built-in reason to return — the antidote to the 2% / 40% leak.

The Solution · 03
The evidence · why we target activation

The drop is at getting data in.

The activation wall
Only ~39% of trials get data in; nearly everyone who does reaches an insight. Fix the wall, not the last step.
It never self-corrects
Data-import sits at a ~40% ceiling across 13 months — it won't rise on its own.
What the on-ramp targets
The sample-data on-ramp aims to lift activation past its ceiling — the M5 experiment. (target)

The Solution · 06
Design principles

Four rules the flow obeys.

One job per screen. Each screen asks for exactly one thing.
Value before asks. Show the insight before requesting anything.
Personalize early. The goal tap tailors the very first insight.
One well-timed nudge. A single weekly prompt — never spam.
The Solution · 07
How we'll know it worked

One number: reached the Aha.

Primary metric: % of new trial users who reach their first modeling output within 7 days (today ~37%). Guardrail: trial→paid conversion must not drop — we activate real value, not vanity taps. This is the leading indicator the M5 experiment moves.
