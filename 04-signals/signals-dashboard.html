The correlation test

Both indexed to 100 at Oct '23 (single axis — no dual scale)
Financial Modeling Usage % Trial-to-Paid Conversion
They're decoupled. Modeling usage climbs to ~187 (nearly 2× its base) while conversion never leaves the 97–108 band. Pushing modeling depth did not move conversion — so the 2% is a structural ceiling, not an activation-depth problem.

Correlation, plotted directly

Each dot = one month · dashed line = best fit · hover for values
r ≈ 0.24 — weak and not statistically significant (n = 13; the p = 0.05 threshold is ~0.55, and r² ≈ 0.06). The best-fit line is nearly flat: across the full modeling range (11% → 58%), predicted conversion moves only ~1.96% → 2.01%. Modeling usage does not predict conversion — the 2% is a structural ceiling. Caveat: a user-level cut (do users who reach a modeling output convert higher than those who don't?) could still find a within-ceiling effect this monthly aggregate can't see.

Confirm / update the hypothesis

Module 1's "the leak is at conversion/activation" is partly confirmed — activation is weak (Import stuck at a ceiling). But the correlation test sharpens it: deepening modeling usage historically did not move conversion, so the 2% is a structural ceiling (pricing / paywall timing / packaging). Experiment priority splits: (a) lift Import Usage to grow the North Star pool, and (b) run a separate structural conversion experiment — you can't convert up by pushing modeling depth alone.
