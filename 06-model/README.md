## The exercise — Build your pricing recommendation

### Step 1 · Assess your monetization stage

| Field | Decision |
|---|---|
| **Current stage** | **Stage 2 — Revenue Expansion.** Value is proven ($10M ARR), but the structural pricing decisions (paywall placement, conversion path) haven't been optimized. |
| **Evidence 1** | $10M ARR confirms PMF — Stage 1 (Value Creation) is complete. |
| **Evidence 2** | 2% trial→paid conversion is a structural ceiling. Module 4 showed Modeling Usage nearly doubled (index 100 → 187) while conversion never left the 97–108 band (r ≈ 0.24, not significant). The cap is in pricing/paywall/packaging — Stage 2 work. |

### Step 2 · Select your pricing model

| Field | Decision |
|---|---|
| **Recommended model** | **Freemium (reverse-trial).** FinWise delivers value continuously (ongoing financial management), but users need to experience the Aha moment before they'll pay. A free tier drives adoption and habit; the paid tier captures committed users. The reverse-trial variant gives 14 days of full access so users feel premium value first. |
| **Key trade-off** | A pure subscription protects ARPU but leaves the funnel broken at 2%. Trading short-term revenue for a permanent free tier gives users time to build the habit that makes paying inevitable, and creates a compounding organic base that reduces paid-acquisition dependence. |

### Step 3 · Make your recommendation

| Field | Decision |
|---|---|
| **Recommendation** | Move from a time-limited trial to a reverse-trial freemium model. Every new user gets 14 days of full premium, then drops to a permanent free tier (import + basic modeling). Advanced features (forecasting, consolidation, team seats) gate behind paid. The paywall lands where engaged users feel they need more. |
| **Justification (M4)** | Targets **Get-Started Import Usage %** (Leading 1), stuck at ~40%. Removing the time wall lets more users reach their first modeling output (the North Star). Conversions should cluster at the feature boundary — where users outgrow basic modeling. |
| **The pricing bet** | **Packaging matched to value** — validating that the free/paid line sits where users feel they need more. Signal: conversions cluster tightly at the boundary. If diffuse, iterate the boundary, not the price. |

### Step 4 · The memo (summary)

**Recommendation: Reverse-Trial Freemium.** FinWise's 2% trial→paid conversion is a structural ceiling (Module 4 evidence), not a price or activation problem. Move the paywall from a time boundary to a feature boundary: 14-day premium → permanent free tier → paid advanced features. Validate with a cohort-based test at 30/60 days, guardrailing ARPU and Import Usage %. This is not a price change — it's a packaging restructure. The paid plan's price stays unchanged; what changes is *when and where* users encounter it.
