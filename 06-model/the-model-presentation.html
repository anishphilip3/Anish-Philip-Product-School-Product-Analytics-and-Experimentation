Step 2 · Pricing Model

Freemium (Reverse-Trial)

Choose the model that fits how FinWise delivers value, not the one that sounds right for fintech.

Current model

Subscription (Trial)

Time-limited trial → paid. Produces 2% conversion. 98% of users leave when the clock runs out, before building the habit that makes paying feel natural.

Recommended

Freemium (Reverse-Trial)

14 days of full premium, then a permanent free tier with import + basic modeling. The paid tier captures users who've crossed the value threshold. Habit before money.

Key trade-off
A pure subscription protects ARPU but leaves the funnel broken at 2%. Trading short-term revenue for a permanent free tier gives users time to build the habit that makes paying inevitable — and creates a compounding organic base that reduces paid-acquisition dependence.

Step 3 · The Recommendation

Shift the paywall to where value is felt

The single most important pricing change FinWise should make to address its 2% conversion ceiling.

Day 1–14

Full premium access. Every new user experiences forecasting, consolidation, and team features.

Day 15+

Drop to free tier. Import + basic modeling stay free forever. Advanced features gate behind paid.

The paywall

Sits at the feature boundary, not a time boundary. Users upgrade because they need more, not because a clock ran out.

M4 Leading indicator targeted
Get-Started Import Usage % — stuck at ~40%. Removing the time wall lets more users cross the activation threshold and reach their first modeling output (the North Star). The paywall lands where engaged users feel they need more, so conversions should cluster tightly at the boundary.
The Bet

Packaging matched to value

Not a price change — a packaging restructure. Where the paywall sits matters more than what the price is.

Hypothesis

If we move the paywall...

If we replace the time-limited trial with a reverse-trial freemium model, then trial-to-paid conversion will exceed the 2% structural ceiling, measured by conversion rate at 30 and 60 days.

Validation signal

Cluster at the boundary

Conversions cluster tightly where users outgrow basic modeling. If they cluster → the line is right, scale. If they're diffuse → iterate the boundary, not the price.

Guardrails

What we protect

ARPU must not significantly decline. Free-tier users must show higher Import Usage % than current trial users at the same tenure. Read at 8 weeks.

The Memo — In Brief

What goes to leadership

The memo distilled to what the leadership team needs to decide.

Reverse-Trial Freemium

Move from a time-limited trial to a reverse-trial freemium model. The paywall moves from a time boundary to a feature boundary. Validate with a cohort test targeting >2% conversion at 30/60 days.

  • Problem: 2% conversion is a structural ceiling, not a price or activation problem (M4 evidence)
  • Stage: Revenue Expansion — the model and paywall placement are the levers
  • Model: Freemium fits how FinWise delivers value (continuous, habit-forming)
  • Change: 14-day premium → permanent free tier (import + basic modeling) → paid advanced features
  • Targets: Get-Started Import Usage % (M4 Leading 1, stuck at ~40%)
  • Bet type: Packaging matched to value — watch for conversion clustering at the boundary
  • Not doing: Changing the price. This restructures when and where users hit the paywall
